Privacy Policy

Effective [[PUBLISH_DATE]] · Last updated [[PUBLISH_DATE]]

The short version.

Flagful has no accounts or adverts. Your drawings, scores and progress are stored on your device and are not sent to us. The app does not collect gameplay analytics or automatically send crash or performance reports through third-party reporting services. It contacts Expo to check for updates. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send.

There is a shorter version written for children at What Flagful knows about you.

This policy explains how Flagful (the “app”) handles information. Flagful is published by [[LEGAL_ENTITY]] (“we”, “us”), [[POSTAL_ADDRESS]].

1 What the app does not collect

You can play without giving us your name, email address or date of birth. The app has:

  • No account, username, password or sign-in
  • No request for your location
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photo library or contacts
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No collection of biometric, payment or financial details
  • No chat, messaging or drawing-sharing feature
  • Flag search stays on your device. Search filters the country list locally. Neither your search words nor search activity are sent to us.

Analytics and error reporting

Flagful does not collect gameplay analytics or automatically send crash or performance reports through third-party reporting services. This applies to everyone, including children. Gameplay events are not saved for later transmission to those services.

Your operating system or app store may separately collect diagnostics under its own privacy settings and policies.

2 What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device:

  • The flags you have drawn and coloured
  • Your scores, stars, streaks and lesson progress
  • Your settings, such as sound, music and haptics

The app does not upload these to us. Depending on your device settings, they may be included in a Google, iCloud or computer backup. We cannot access those backups. Deleting the app removes its local data; a backup copy may remain until you delete it separately.

3 Information sent outside the app

3.1 App update checks

Flagful uses Expo's update service to receive fixes and content updates. When the app checks for an update, it sends technical information such as the platform, app runtime version, project identifier and a random installation token. Expo also receives the network request, including the connecting IP address. These requests do not include your drawings, scores, lesson activity or search words. See Expo's privacy explanation.

3.2 When you contact us

If you email us for support or make a privacy request, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and handle your request. Please send only what is needed to explain the problem. Parents and guardians can contact us on a child's behalf.

3.3 Visiting this website

This website has no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies added by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information, including your IP address, when your browser requests a page or asset. Its handling of that information is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

4 Service providers

Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) delivers app updates. GitHub hosts this website. Our email provider, [[EMAIL_PROVIDER]], handles the messages you send us. Device backups are managed separately by you and your backup provider. We do not sell personal information or share it for behavioural advertising.

6 Children

Flagful is made for children and adults. The same privacy protections apply to everyone. We do not ask players for their age or require an account. Gameplay analytics and third-party crash and performance reporting are off for all players. There are no adverts or in-app sharing controls.

Drawings, progress and search words are not uploaded to us. Device backups and technical update requests work as described in sections 2 and 3.

Parents and guardians: you can contact us about your child's privacy or ask us to delete personal information you or your child have sent to us. Write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. You do not need to send your child's drawings or other unnecessary personal details.

7 How long data is kept

  • Drawings, progress and settings: stored on your device until removed or the app is deleted. Backup copies follow your backup settings and your provider's retention rules.
  • Support and privacy correspondence: [[CORRESPONDENCE_RETENTION]].
  • Technical update records: [[UPDATE_RECORD_RETENTION]].
  • Website request information: retained by GitHub as described in its privacy statement linked in section 3.3.

8 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • To delete local app data: delete the app. Remove any backup copy separately through your device or backup provider.
  • For information we or our service providers hold: contact [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We will respond within the time limits required by applicable law. We may need limited information to verify and locate your request.

We cannot view or remotely delete drawings and progress stored on your device or in your own backups.

9 International transfers

Expo and GitHub are based in the United States. Information handled by our service providers may be processed outside your country. [[PROCESSING_LOCATIONS_AND_TRANSFER_SAFEGUARDS]]

10 Security

App update requests and access to this website use HTTPS. Your drawings and progress remain in your device's app storage, subject to your backup settings. Please avoid sending sensitive information in a support email unless it is needed to resolve your request.

11 Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its “last updated” date before the change takes effect. Where required, we will also notify you and obtain your consent, or a parent's or guardian's consent, before starting the new collection.

12 Contact
