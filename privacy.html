Privacy Policy

Effective [[PUBLISH_DATE]] · Last updated [[PUBLISH_DATE]]

The short version.

Flagful has no accounts and no server of its own. Your drawings, your scores and your progress are never sent to us. The app does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix bugs and understand which lessons work. It has no ads and asks for no personal details. Its only text field is the flag search, and what you type there never leaves your device.

There is a shorter version written for children at What Flagful knows about you.

This policy explains how Flagful (the “app”) handles information. Flagful is published by [[LEGAL_ENTITY]] (“we”, “us”), [[POSTAL_ADDRESS]].

1 What Flagful never collects

Flagful does not ask for, receive or store any of the following. There is no mechanism in the app to provide them:

  • No account, username, password or sign-in of any kind
  • No name, email address, phone number or date of birth
  • No location, GPS or approximate position
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos or contacts
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising of any kind
  • No biometrics and no payment or financial details
  • No messaging between users, and no user-generated content shared to us
  • Nothing you type is collected. There is no comment box, feedback form or chat. The only text field is the flag search: it filters the list on your device, and we record only that a search was made, never the words.

2 What stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us. If your device's own backup is switched on (Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone and iPad), it is included in that backup, which is held by your device's provider under your account. We cannot see those backups. Uninstalling the app deletes the copy on the device:

  • The flags you have drawn and coloured
  • Your scores, stars, streaks and lesson progress
  • Your settings, such as sound, music and haptics

3 What is sent off the device

Three things leave your device. Each is described in full below.

3.1 Usage analytics (PostHog)

Flagful records how the app is used so we can see which lessons are confusing, where people give up, and which features are unused. Events describe actions, not people — for example that a lesson step was completed, that a hint was used, or that a drawing was submitted and what score it received.

Alongside each event we send:

  • A persistent pseudonymous device identifier — a random value generated on the device the first time the app runs. It is not derived from your device's hardware identifiers and is not linked to your name or to any account, but it is stable across sessions, which means it counts as an online identifier under the GDPR and a persistent identifier under COPPA.
  • Basic device context: app version, platform (Android or iOS), operating system version, device language, app language, and whether the device is a phone or a tablet. The app deliberately removes the time zone and locale that the analytics library would otherwise attach on its own.

PostHog is configured to discard the IP address your device connects from, so no location is worked out from it — not even a country or a city.

We do not send your drawings, your images, or any text. Analytics are disabled entirely in development builds. Data is sent to PostHog's European servers (eu.i.posthog.com).

3.2 Crash and performance reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or hits an error, a diagnostic report is sent so we can fix it. It contains the error message and stack trace, the app version, the operating system and device model, and a trail of which screens were visited beforehand. A sample of sessions (20% of traces and 10% of performance profiles) is also sent to find slowdowns.

The app is explicitly configured not to attach personal data to these reports: request headers and user context are switched off, and Sentry is set not to store the IP address a report arrives from. Reports go to Sentry's European region.

3.3 App update checks (Expo)

When the app starts it asks Expo's update service, which delivers our updates, whether a newer version of the app's content is available. That request includes an installation identifier, the app and runtime version, the platform and the operating system version. It is how bug fixes reach you without waiting for an app store release.

3.4 When you share a drawing

Tapping share opens your device's own share sheet with a picture of your drawing. You choose where it goes. The image is handed to the app you pick and is governed by that app's privacy policy from then on. We never receive it and keep no copy.

6 Children

During closed testing. Test versions of Flagful are available only to adults we invite. Testers agree, when they join, to the usage analytics and crash reports described in §3, and are asked not to let children use test versions. The rest of this section describes how the released app treats children; we will revise it before public release.

Flagful is suitable for children and we expect children to use it. We have designed it so that using it reveals as little as possible about the person holding the device.

  • There is no advertising in Flagful, and no behavioural or personalised advertising of any kind.
  • Nothing a child types is collected. The only text field is the flag search, and what is typed there stays on the device.
  • Drawings and progress stay on the device and are never uploaded.
  • We do not knowingly build profiles of individual children, and we do not use the data described in §3 to target or advertise to anyone.

Parents and guardians: you may ask what data associated with a device we hold, ask us to delete it, or tell us to stop collecting it. Write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. Please see the honest limitation described in §8 about pseudonymous records.

7 How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it in the app or uninstall Flagful. We set no time limit because we cannot see it.
  • Usage analytics: [[POSTHOG_RETENTION]], after which records are deleted.
  • Crash and performance reports: [[SENTRY_RETENTION]], after which records are deleted.

8 Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • To delete everything held on your device: uninstall the app. This is immediate and needs no request to us. If your device backup is on, also delete Flagful's data from that backup in your Google or Apple account settings.
  • To stop analytics and crash reports: uninstall the app. There is no switch for this inside the app yet. Testers can also leave the test from the app's Google Play page.
  • For anything held by our processors: write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] and we will respond within one month.

A limitation we would rather state than hide. The analytics and crash data described in §3 is tied to a random device identifier and to no other information about you. If you write to us, we generally have no way to work out which of those anonymous records is yours unless you can tell us the identifier. In that situation we cannot honestly promise to find and delete your specific record, and we will tell you so rather than claim otherwise. Uninstalling the app stops any further data being sent.

9 International transfers

Analytics and crash data stay in the European Union. App update checks (§3.3) are handled by Expo in the United States; that transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10 Security

All data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections. Because Flagful holds no accounts, no passwords and no directly identifying information, the consequences of any incident are limited by design.

11 Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and change the “last updated” date above before the change reaches you.
