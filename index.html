Learn the flags of the world by drawing them

Flagful teaches you the flags of the world one stripe at a time — what each colour means, why the symbols are there, and how to draw the whole thing from memory.

✦ Built to know as little as possible

  • No accounts and no sign-in — open the app and start drawing
  • No adverts, of any kind, anywhere
  • No gameplay analytics
  • Your drawings and progress are not uploaded to us
  • Nothing you type is collected — flag search stays on your device

The full privacy policy sets out exactly what the app sends and why. There is also a short version written for children.
