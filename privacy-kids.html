What Flagful knows about you

The short version, for anyone who would rather not read the long one.

Your drawings stay with you

Your drawings and progress are saved on your phone or tablet. The app does not send them to us. If your device makes a backup, it may save a copy there too.

We never ask who you are

Flagful never asks for your name, your age, your email, or where you live. The only place to type is the flag search, and whatever you type there stays on your device, so you can't tell us by accident.

Your play stays private

Flagful does not tell us which lessons you play, what you search for, or what scores you get. It does not send reports to a crash-reporting service when something goes wrong.

The app checks for updates

Flagful asks an update service if there are fixes to download. It sends a few technical details to get the right update for your device. It does not send your drawings or lesson progress.

There are no adverts

Flagful has no adverts in it at all, and we never sell anything about you to anybody.

You can delete your drawings

If you delete the app, its drawings and progress go with it. If a grown-up has backups switched on, they can delete Flagful's copy from the backup too.

Got a question? Ask a grown-up to email us at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]], or read the full privacy policy, which explains all of this in much more detail. If a grown-up emails us, we can see their email address and what they send so we can reply.
