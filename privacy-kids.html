What Flagful knows about you

The short version, for anyone who would rather not read the long one.

Your drawings stay with you

Every flag you colour in stays on your own phone or tablet. We never see it. Nobody else sees it either, unless you tap share and choose to send it to someone yourself.

We never ask who you are

Flagful never asks for your name, your age, your email, or where you live. There is nowhere in the app to type any of that, so you can't tell us by accident.

We count things, not people

The app does tell us things like “someone finished the Japan lesson” or “someone used a hint here”. That helps us make the lessons better. It doesn't tell us who that someone was.

When something breaks, we hear about it

If the app crashes, it sends us a note explaining what went wrong so we can fix it. The note is about the app, not about you.

There are no adverts

Flagful has no adverts in it at all, and we never sell anything about you to anybody.

You can delete everything

If you delete the app, everything it kept goes with it. You can also clear your progress in the app's settings whenever you like.
